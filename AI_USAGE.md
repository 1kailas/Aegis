# AI Usage Record — Aegis AI

This document details the AI architecture, models, and usage protocols governing **Aegis AI**.

---

## Architectural Role of AI in Aegis

In accordance with Aegis's core architectural principle:
> *"Aegis monitors and orchestrates. Deterministic controllers enforce safety. AI investigates and engineers fixes. Independent verification determines whether the fix actually worked."*

AI models in Aegis are strictly restricted from directly controlling critical safety mechanisms or executing arbitrary commands on the host. Instead, AI serves two distinct roles:

1. **Root-Cause Investigation (Aegis Intelligence Plane)**:
   - Analyzes telemetry anomalies, kernel messages, and container logs to diagnose failure modes.
   - Operated via a 3-tier fallback architecture:
     - **Tier 1 (Local)**: Local Ollama running `qwen2.5:1.5b` for zero-latency, private offline diagnosis.
     - **Tier 2 (Cloud)**: Google Gemini (`gemini-flash-latest`) for advanced reasoning when local models are unavailable or encounter ambiguous failure signatures. Strictly budget-capped with hourly rate limits.
     - **Tier 3 (Deterministic Heuristics)**: 100% offline rule-based diagnosis ensuring continuous operation even during total network partition.
   - All diagnostic outputs are scrubbed for credentials and validated against typed Pydantic schemas (`DiagnosisSchema`) labeled as `ASSESSMENT`.

2. **Complex Software & Configuration Repair (Devin Engineering Plane)**:
   - Complex code, configuration, and dependency fixes are delegated to **Devin** as the designated intelligent repair engineer.
   - Dispatches structured `RepairTask` payloads with reproduction steps, acceptance criteria, scope boundaries, and security constraints.
   - Devin-generated patches are never applied directly to production workloads; they must undergo isolated workspace cloning, scope validation, and sandbox test execution.

## Human Review & Audit Trail

- Every remediation proposal generated by an AI model is recorded in the SQLite `remediation_actions` audit table with timestamp, risk category, and proposed parameters.
- In `operator_approval` mode, proposals remain pending until an operator explicitly approves them via the API or dashboard.
- In `policy_autonomous` mode, only allowlisted low-risk actions (`restart_container`, `set_backpressure`, `increase_sampling`) are executed automatically. Prohibited actions (`rm -rf`, raw shell piping) are unconditionally blocked by deterministic policy code.
- Every pull request created by Aegis contains complete validation logs, test outputs, exit codes, and an independent recovery verification report.
